# Campus Rhythm — User Guide

Live app: **https://campus-rhythm-one.vercel.app**

This guide walks you through the main flow: **sign in → import → plan → do → review**.

---

## 1. Sign in

- Open the website and choose **Continue with Google**, or use your email and password.
- Your plan is saved to your account and syncs across web and iOS.
- New here? You'll be guided to **import your existing commitments first** — goals and rest preferences can come later.

## 2. Import your commitments

From **Import / Student import guide**:

- **Canvas deadlines** — connect your Canvas calendar feed. Deadlines appear for preview.
- **Class or event calendars (ICS)** — choose a timetable export (e.g. HKUST Timetable Planner) or another ICS file.
- **Review before saving** — preview the records, select what to keep, check dates and times, then confirm.
- Imports are **snapshots**, not continuous sync. You can remove a whole batch or series later, and restore it if needed.

Tip: date-only deadlines stay as dates; you choose whether and when to allocate preparation time.

## 3. See your plan

- **Calendar** — Month view shows course codes/sections; select a day for its agenda with times. Week and Day views share the same commitments.
- **Today** — one proactive next step based on deadlines, remaining effort and your rest windows.
- Exams and deadlines stand out in the **All day & due** band.

## 4. Add a task and get a suggested time

1. **Add task** with a title, due date and estimated minutes.
2. Ask **Rhythm** for a suggested time (or use the local planner).
3. Review the draft: it checks fixed classes, rest time and workload.
4. **Confirm** to save — or edit first. Nothing changes without your confirmation.

Heavy tasks can be split into multiple sessions across days.

## 5. Do, undo, review

- **Done** records actual completion and minutes — scheduled time is not counted as finished work.
- **Undo** reverts a change, including a completed task or a saved plan.
- **Weekly review** shows what you actually completed and how your week is trending.

## 6. iOS extras

- Home Screen widgets show your next task at a glance; Live Activities track an ongoing focus session.
- Notifications are opt-in, with quiet hours and privacy controls.
- Apple Health (sleep, steps, exercise minutes) is **read-only and stays on your device**.

## Privacy & boundaries

- Each account is isolated with row-level security; your plan is private to you.
- The AI returns drafts only — it never writes to your calendar directly.
- Health data is not uploaded to the cloud or sent to the AI.
- The app is a prototype: continuous external sync (Outlook, live Canvas updates) and measured wellbeing outcomes are not claimed.

## Feedback

Found something confusing? Use the short in-app survey link — it takes about 4 minutes and directly shapes the next iteration.
