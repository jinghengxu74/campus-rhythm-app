# Campus Rhythm

**A proactive planner for people with tight schedules — understand your day before it runs you.**

🌐 **Try it live: https://campus-rhythm-one.vercel.app**

📖 **How to use it: [User Guide](GUIDE.md)**

---

## What is Campus Rhythm?

Campus Rhythm brings your classes, deadlines, commitments and personal goals into **one reviewed plan**. It helps you choose a feasible next step, and builds a rhythm around what you actually do — not just what you planned.

Knowing a deadline is not the same as having time to prepare for it. Campus Rhythm treats a deadline and the work required to meet it as different things, and helps you fit real work into a real day.

Started from a student project at **HKUST**, built for a universal problem: scattered commitments and too little time.

## What you can do

- **Import what already exists** — preview Canvas deadlines and class/event calendars (ICS), select what to keep, review before saving.
- **Get a feasible next step** — an AI-assisted planner weighs due dates, remaining effort, fixed commitments and your chosen rest windows. Every suggestion is a **draft you review**; nothing is saved without your confirmation.
- **Follow through** — record Done and actual minutes, split heavy tasks into sessions, keep history, and **undo** any change.
- **Review your rhythm** — weekly reviews based on what you actually did.
- **Stay close to the day** — iOS Home Screen widgets, Live Activities and local notifications keep the next step visible.
- **Respect your privacy** — read-only Apple Health (sleep, steps, exercise minutes) stays on device and is not sent to the cloud or the AI.

## Quick start

1. Open **https://campus-rhythm-one.vercel.app**
2. Sign in with your Google account (or email).
3. Import a calendar or Canvas feed, or add a task manually.
4. Ask for a suggested time, review the draft, confirm — and undo anytime.

Full walkthrough with tips: **[GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md)**

## Honest status

Campus Rhythm is a working prototype, not an App Store release. Outcomes like reduced stress or better grades are not claimed — they need real pilot studies. Feedback is collected through an optional short survey linked in the app.
